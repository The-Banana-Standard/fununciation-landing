How a session works

Three steps, again and again.

1 · You pick the sound

Choose the sound, the positions and a game. It takes seconds, even with a child already in the chair.

2 · The child says the word

A picture and a word card come up. You judge the production. The app never grades speech and never moves on without you.

3 · Every try earns something

One quick tap, flick or drag. Some tries land, some miss in a silly way, and the child keeps something every time.

Progress tracking

Scores the child never sees.

Mark each try with one tap, or fill in the report from memory after the round. Results live on your setup screen, never on the game. No red, no green, no failing grade.
